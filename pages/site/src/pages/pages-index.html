Your pages → How publishing works

Who can open a page

A published page is private the moment it exists. Its owner opens one of two doors, and closes it again whenever they like.

  • You — the account that published it, always.
  • People you name. Add someone's email address; they sign in and the service checks the address against your list. Forwarding the page address to anyone else achieves nothing.
  • Anyone holding a sharing link. One link per page, shown once, no sign-in. Turn it off and the link dies immediately.

Removing someone also destroys the pass they are holding, so the page closes on their next request rather than at the end of it.
