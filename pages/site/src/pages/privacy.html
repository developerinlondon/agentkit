Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 August 2026

This policy covers the website at agentkit.sbs and the AgentKit Pages publishing service. The agentkit toolkit itself is open source software that runs on your own machine; it reports nothing back to us, and this policy does not apply to it.

Who we are

These services are operated by NSM Technologies Limited, registered in England and Wales under company number 16748005, with its registered office at 7 Sanderling Close, Bicester, England, OX26 6WF. We are the data controller for the personal information described here. Contact us at info@nsmtechnologies.co.uk.

Signing in

Publishing a page requires an account. We do not operate our own password database for it — sign-in is handled by our identity service at auth.assay.rs, which can in turn authenticate you through Google.

When you sign in we store an account record holding the identifier the identity service issues for you, your email address and your display name. We also store session records, and the long-lived device credentials the command line tool uses so that you do not have to sign in on every publish.

Data originating from Google Sign-In

If you authenticate through Google, the only Google account data that reaches us is your account identifier, your email address and your name. We use it solely to identify you and to attribute the pages you publish. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell or transfer it, and we do not use it to train machine learning models.

You can revoke access at any time from your Google account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. That stops future sign-ins through Google but does not by itself delete your AgentKit account.

Pages you publish

We store the content of the pages you publish, along with their slug, size and timestamps, so we can serve them. Published pages are served at pages.agentkit.sbs and are reachable by anyone who has the link. Do not publish anything through this service that you would not want a stranger to read.

You are responsible for the personal data you place inside a page you publish. For that content you are the controller and we act as your processor.

Why we may lawfully do this

We process account data and page content to perform the contract by which we provide the service to you. We process security and rate-limiting data on the basis of our legitimate interests in keeping the service available and preventing abuse.

Logs

Our network provider records technical information needed to serve requests securely, such as IP addresses, timestamps and requested paths. These are retained briefly for operational and anti-abuse purposes and then discarded.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with providers processing it on our behalf under contract: our network, storage and database provider, and the identity service described above. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where the law requires it, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

International transfers

Our providers operate global networks, so your information may be processed outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens we rely on the safeguards UK data protection law recognises, such as adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your account record and your published pages while your account exists. Sessions and device credentials expire on their own. If you delete a page we remove its content. If you delete your account we remove your account record, your device credentials and your published pages within 30 days, except where the law requires us to retain something.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you may ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to restrict or object to our use of it, and ask us to transfer it to another organisation in a portable format. Email info@nsmtechnologies.co.uk. We respond within one month and there is no charge.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK supervisory authority, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Where a change materially affects data we already hold about you, we will contact you.
